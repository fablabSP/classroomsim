ClassroomSim 3.2 demo

The real app on three screens: an evaluator's laptop, a second evaluator's phone and a participant's phone, all connected. 8 sample participants and a second evaluator have joined. Nothing leaves this page, and Reset starts again.

Code ······ · Evaluator code ····-····-··
  1. Evaluator laptop: the join QR pops up as soon as a session is created. Close it, then follow “What to do now”. Confirm trainers, deal role cards, Start. Or press Quick start.
  2. Participant phone: the role card arrives face down. Tap it to flip it open.
  3. Either evaluator screen, Run: pick a learner, pick a card, Deal. That phone shakes and the card is dealt face down.
  4. Evaluator phone, Observe: tap ⚑ Flag, then what it was. End the round, then Review and Draft from notes.
